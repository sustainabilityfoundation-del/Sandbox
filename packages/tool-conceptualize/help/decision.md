**Decide** opens a decision with one outcome per alternative (accept, reject, defer, each with a reason)
and a required rationale. Editors **propose**; recording accepted or rejected outcomes is an approval
gate for **reviewers and administrators** (proposed decisions show a **Record** button for them).
Two people are involved: reviewers record but do not create decisions (*propose as an editor, then
record*); only an administrator may propose and record in one step (**Record decision** in the dialog). When
recorded, accepted alternatives become *selected*; rejected ones are kept with the rejection.

To revisit a decision when constraints change, record a new decision that supersedes it; the old record
stays readable. The assistant can only propose decisions (*Pursue CON-019*).
