title: Concept Explorer (tree and graph)
kind: guide
summary: Search and filter Concepts, navigate the hierarchy with alternative decompositions and alternative sets, or see relationships as a graph.
keywords: [explorer, tree, graph, hierarchy, search, filter, decomposition]
related: [conceptualize.structure, conceptualize.alternatives]
---
- **Tree**: top-level Concepts with their children (contains). When a parent has several
  **decompositions** (alternative decomposition structures), children are grouped by decomposition.
  **Alternative sets** appear under their parent with each alternative's status. A Concept that is part
  of several parents is marked *shared*. **AI** marks Concepts created from an assistant proposal.
- **Graph** (toolbar **Graph** or the Tree/Graph switch): the open Concept with its neighbours (or the
  project when nothing is open). Solid lines: contains; dotted: alternatives; dashed: other
  relationships. Nodes are keyboard-focusable; press Enter to open.
- **Search** and the **maturity filter** show a flat list of matches.

Concept decomposition does not have to match the CAD assembly or SysML containment.
