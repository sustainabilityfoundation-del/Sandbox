title: Getting started with Conceptualize
kind: tutorial
summary: Describe an idea in your own words, review the interpreted Concept, decompose it, generate alternatives and link an implementation.
keywords: [start, first concept, tutorial, natural language]
related: [conceptualize.concept.create, conceptualize.alternatives, conceptualize.implementation]
---
1. Choose **New concept** in the toolbar and describe the idea, for example *A compact mechanical
   actuator capable of delivering 500 N of linear force within a 100 mm envelope.*
2. Check the interpreted name, functions and constraints (quantities with units). They are recognized
   deterministically, without a language model. Choose **Create Concept**: it is saved as revision 1,
   maturity *idea*.
3. With the Concept open, choose **Add child** to decompose it into functions or parts.
4. Ask the assistant (ChatGPT panel, a scripted demo): *Develop three alternative concepts for …*.
   Review the preview and choose **Apply**. The alternatives appear in the tree under the parent and in
   **Alternatives**.
5. In **Alternatives**, compare the options in the trade study and **Decide**. A reviewer records
   accepted or rejected outcomes.
6. In the Concept workspace, tab **Implementations**, add an implementation and **Link artifact**
   (for example CAD-120). Replacing the artifact later does not change the Concept.
