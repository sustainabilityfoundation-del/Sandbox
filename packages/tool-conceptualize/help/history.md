Revisions never change after they are written. The history lists revisions, maturity changes,
relationships, decisions, implementations and links with time, person, reason and provenance.
Assistant changes show *From assistant PRP-…*. Earlier revisions stay readable through the API
(`?revision=`) and comparable (`/compare?from=&to=`).
