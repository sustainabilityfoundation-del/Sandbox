summary: Orbit, pan, zoom and fit with the pointer, buttons or keys; pick faces; read stable face names and properties.

- **Pointer:** drag to orbit, right-drag or two-finger drag to pan, wheel or pinch to zoom. Click a
  face to select it; it is highlighted and shown in **Inspect**.
- **Buttons** (top right): isometric, front, top and right views, zoom in and out, show or hide
  edges. **Fit** is in the toolbar (F).
- **Keyboard** (focus the view): arrow keys orbit, + and − zoom, F fits, Escape clears the
  selection. Faces can also be chosen in **Inspect → Select a face**.

Faces have **stable names** from design intent: `ext-5/cap-start`, `ext-5/cap-end`,
`ext-5/side:ln-8` (the side generated by sketch line ln-8), `rev-9/side:arc-4`. Names stay the same
when dimensions change. A face that cannot be traced to a feature gets an unstable name `face#n`
and is marked as such.
