- **Search** looks in keys, titles, statements and rationale.
- **Lifecycle**, type and **Archived** filters narrow the list; **Filter** (toolbar) limits it to the
  selected component's allocations.
- **Columns** chooses what is shown, including **Origin** (user constraint, AI-proposed choice that a person applied, or derived; "Unknown" when none was recorded, never guessed); **SysML column** adds SysML usages.
- **Save view** keeps the current filters and columns; **Saved views** restores them.
- **Bulk edit** applies one change to several requirements; you choose whether it is all-or-nothing.
- Pages load as you scroll, so large projects stay responsive.
