title: Writing good requirements
kind: guide
summary: Practical guidance for clear, verifiable requirement statements.
keywords: [writing, style, shall, verifiable, singular, unambiguous]
related: [requirements.quality, requirements.requirement.create]
---

- State one obligation per requirement.
- Make it verifiable: give values with units and tolerances, and name the conditions.
- Keep the statement normative; put the reason in **Rationale** and discussion in **Comments**.
- Avoid vague words such as "fast", "user-friendly" or "as appropriate" unless they are defined.
- Record how it will be verified (method and acceptance criteria).

These are conventions, not rules enforced on every project; your project's quality conventions decide
what the checks flag.
