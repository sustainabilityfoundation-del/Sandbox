On the **Baselines** page choose **Create baseline** (reviewer or administrator). A requirements
baseline pins requirement revisions, relationship revisions and collection structure; it can never be
changed afterwards.

Open a baseline to see that historical state, clearly labelled read-only, or compare it with the working
state or another baseline. To compare two revisions of one requirement, use its **History** tab.

Requirements baselines are separate from the workspace baselines in the header's **Baselines** menu,
which pin the whole engineering definition, including requirement revisions.
