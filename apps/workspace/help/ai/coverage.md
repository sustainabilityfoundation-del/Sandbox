Open **AI capability coverage** from the command palette (Ctrl+K) or **What can the assistant do?** in
the assistant panel. Each capability shows its implementation status (implemented, experimental, mock)
and the assistant's coverage: shared operation, equivalent, partial, no AI path, or restricted.

Restricted capabilities are deliberately unavailable to the assistant: approvals, identity and
subscription changes, destructive demo controls, and features that are not built yet.
