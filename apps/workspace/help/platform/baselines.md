**Baselines → Create baseline** pins the exact revision of every object, scenario and requirement in
the configuration, and the relationships between them. **Compare with a baseline** lists what changed
since, with old and new values.

Select an object to see in the inspector who owns it, which saved results would become stale if it
changed, and which baselines pin it.
