Every requirement, concept, SysML model, CAD document, task and document belongs to a **project**. Project
Management keeps the list of projects, who is a member with which role, and a **registry** that gives every
artifact in the project one global identity, whichever module owns it. The modules keep their own data; the
registry stores references and summaries only.

- **Project Home** lists your projects and the ones you opened recently, and creates new ones ([Project Home](help:projects.home)).
- **New project** starts a genuinely empty project: no requirements, concepts, models, CAD documents or
  planning items in any module ([New project wizard](help:projects.wizard)).
- The **project switcher** in the header shows the open project; choose it to open Project Home.
- **This project** shows the description, lifecycle status, modules, members, milestones and recent activity,
  and lets administrators archive, restore or delete the project ([Project overview](help:projects.overview-panel)).
- The **product structure** (product, systems, assemblies, parts) belongs to Project Management; other modules
  link to its elements ([Product structure](help:projects.product-structure)).
- The assistant can do the same through the `projects.*` operations ([Assistant operations](help:projects.assistant)).

The example project **ACT100 · ACT-100 Linear actuator** is kept as a fixture: its data and IDs are unchanged.
